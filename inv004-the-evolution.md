# INV004: The evolution

> Published on Nov 25, 2026

Beneath the foundation, just above the ground rule, there's one more layer: **everything can be changed when there is great need.** Even "nothing is owed back". Even this sentence.

## 1. The worry

If everything can change, what can I count on? Rules that bend whenever someone says "great need" sound like no rules at all. Plenty of people have grabbed power by declaring an emergency.

It's a fair worry. It deserves a straight answer.

## 2. Why it sits so low

A design that can't change will break. The world keeps changing: the climate, the machines, what people need. Rules made for one time can trap the next. Many old rules are still followed long after anyone remembers why.

So the evolution sits beneath the foundation. Not because change matters more than gifts, but because without it, even a good foundation could one day turn into a cage.

## 3. Great need, not any need

The word that carries the weight is *great*. Not "someone would like it". Not "it would be easier". Great need means people are going without, getting hurt, or the design simply isn't working.

## 4. Nobody owns the change

Who decides? Not one leader. The same ideas that keep power from piling up help here too:

- **Everything is visible.** A change everyone can see is hard to sneak in.
- **Decide close to home.** Most changes are made by the people they affect.
- **Try small first.** Test a change in one place before it goes everywhere, and keep it easy to undo.

## 5. The ground rule stays beneath

That's why the ground rule is at the very bottom, like dirt beneath grass. Whatever changes, anyone can still walk away. If a change goes wrong, or someone uses "great need" as an excuse, people can leave. Power that needs you to stay doesn't last.

## 6. This design too

The third proposition, mistakes welcome, already says all ten points could use fixing. The evolution says how: when the need is great, in the open, a little at a time.

This blog is an evolving plan. Your corrections are part of it.

## In short

Everything can change when there is great need. Change in the open, start small, keep it easy to undo. And whatever changes, anyone can still walk away.

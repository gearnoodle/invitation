# INV001: Nothing is owed back

> Published on Nov 24, 2026

This is the foundation of the whole design. Every gift, every help, every thanks asks nothing back. It stands on one ground rule: anyone can walk away.

## 1. Why it matters

Look at why people steal, cheat, fight and go to war. Very often, it comes down to one thing: not having enough, or being afraid of not having enough. Land, water, food, money, safety.

When there isn't enough, people keep score. Who gave what. Who owes whom. Even kindness turns into a deal.

## 2. Gifts that became debts

People have tried gift economies many times. Often, the gifts quietly turned into debts. You gave me something, so now I owe you.

That's not a gift. It's a loan with a smile.

So whenever a gift starts to feel like a debt, we come back here: nothing is owed back.

## 3. The ground rule: anyone can walk away

If nothing is owed, nobody is tied to anyone. You can leave any group, any project, any leader, at any time.

- **The good side:** nobody can trap you. Power over people only lasts while they choose to stay.
- **The hard side:** people can also leave when you need them. Some may take and never give. The generous few may end up carrying everyone.

Both sides come together. Freedom to leave is the price, and the protection, of owing nothing.

This takes practice. Most of us grew up keeping score, so giving freely and letting people leave won't feel natural at first. It's worth training our minds for it, gently, a little at a time. That work starts in circle zero, our inner world.

## The ultimate aim: enough

A world like this only works when everyone has enough. Not everything. Enough. That's the ultimate aim of the whole design.

When your food, home, health and safety don't depend on what you give or get, there's nothing to keep score of. Then a gift can finally just be a gift.

## What enough doesn't fix

Not all harm comes from scarcity. Some people who already have plenty still want power, status or to be above others. Whoever controls the "enough" could use it as a weapon. And swapping favours, which keeps some friendships going, may fade.

There's no perfect answer yet, but a few ideas help:

- **The floor belongs to no one.** Food, homes and care don't come through any one leader, so nobody can take them away.
- **Nothing to pile up.** Without money to hoard, there's little to build lasting power on.
- **Everything is visible.** It's hard to grab power when everyone can see what's happening.
- **Decide close to home.** Most decisions are made by the people they affect, not from the top.
- **The three asks.** Don't harm, don't deceive, don't block others.

This is an open question. Better ideas are welcome.

## In short

Nothing is owed back. It stands on the ground rule, anyone can walk away, which keeps everyone free. And it's only possible when everyone has enough, the ultimate aim. That's worth designing for.

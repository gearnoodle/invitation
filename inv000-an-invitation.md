# INV000: An invitation

> Published on Nov 24, 2026

![Mosaic Network Globe Icon 1K.png](https://invitation.mataroa.blog/images/db379617.png)

An invitation to design how all of humanity lives together, on our one shared planet. It's the only one we've got. This is not a plan. Nobody has to follow it. I write it, but anyone is welcome to add an idea, a correction or a post.

## Nine points for anyone who wants to join in

All nine sit inside one ultimate aim: **one day, everyone finally has enough.** That's what this design invites us towards.

![eight-points.png](https://invitation.mataroa.blog/images/315f852a.png)

### The ground rule

> **Anyone can walk away.**
>
> You can leave any group, any project, any leader, at any time. Nobody can trap you, and nobody is bound to stay. Everything else stands on this.

### The evolution

> **Everything can be changed when there is great need.**
>
> Even the foundation above it. Nothing in this design is fixed forever. When there is great need, we change it.

### The foundation

> **Nothing is owed back.**
>
> Every gift, every help, every thanks asks nothing back. In many gift economies, gifts quietly turned into debts. Whenever that starts to happen, we come back here. If you remember only one thing from this blog, remember this. How could a world work like that? **Because one day, everyone finally has enough.** Want to know more? Read [INV001: Nothing is owed back](https://invitation.mataroa.blog/blog/inv001-nothing-is-owed-back/) next.

### Three propositions

1. **No blame.** When something goes wrong, look for causes, not someone to blame.
2. **No credit.** Nobody needs praise, because what we do grows from what others gave us. Nobody owns what we made together.
3. **Mistakes welcome.** We learn mostly from mistakes, our own or someone else's. Even all nine of these points could use fixing, this one included. If so, we change them.

### Three asks

1. **Don't harm.** Not people, not animals, not the Earth we share.
2. **Don't deceive.** Trust is what makes rules unneeded.
3. **Don't block others,** unless they harm or deceive someone. Their life is theirs to design.

Everything else is an invitation. Much of a good life is already here: trees, a long lunch, a friend to sit with.

Each of these nine points will get its own post later.

## The simple thing

We know how to give every person food, warmth, a roof and clean water. We still don't. The problem isn't the people. It's the design: the rules and habits that decide how we share work, money and homes.

## A floor and an open ceiling

Think of a house. The floor is what nobody should ever fall below: enough food, a safe home, care when you're ill. The ceiling is open: above the floor, everything is yours to explore, or ignore.

One day, maybe far in the future, every person, every animal and the Earth itself could have this floor: clean air, clean water, room to live. Nature keeps its own ways, lions included. We just stop making things worse.

## The circles

Picture circles around you, from the inside out. The circles are about effort: zero takes the least effort to change, nine the most. Funnily, zero also takes the most, because everything around us has shaped how we think. That's why it appears twice, just like on a keyboard, where 0 comes after 9. Our minds can hold more than the whole universe. There's no order and no climbing. Reach into whichever one you like.

![circles.png](https://invitation.mataroa.blog/images/bae66774.png)

- **0. Your inner world.** Thoughts, feelings, motivation, inspiration.
- **1. Yourself.** Health, habits, sleep, your body.
- **2. People close to you.** Sharing, resting, asking for help.
- **3. Your street and town.** Benches, gardens, shared tools.
- **4. Everyday systems.** Work, money, healthcare, schools: the rules most of us live inside.
- **5. The crowd.** What most people want, buy and expect. Hard to change, because no one person controls it.
- **6. Human civilisation.** The biggest system we've built: countries, cultures, knowledge, machines, and generations to come.
- **7. The Earth itself.** Air, water, soil, animals, plants. We didn't make them and don't control them, but we can harm or protect them.
- **8. Space within reach.** The Moon, Mars, the space around our planet. Places we may one day visit, and should treat with care.
- **9. The universe.** Far beyond our reach. Not part of the invitation, just something to wonder at.

Every post sits in one of these circles.

Come in, look around, leave whenever you like.
